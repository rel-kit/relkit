**Inspector has no active app build.**

## What happened

The Inspector could not find an active build of the app to run the action against.

## What to do

Wait for the app to finish starting or reloading, then refresh the Inspector and try again.

Reported by `@relkit/diagnostics`, `@relkit/inspector-api`.
