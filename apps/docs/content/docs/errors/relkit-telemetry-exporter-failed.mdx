**Telemetry exporter failed.**

## What happened

A telemetry exporter threw an error while sending, flushing or closing, so some logs, spans or metrics may not have reached it.

## What to do

Check the exporter's endpoint, credentials and network access. Your app keeps running, and other exporters are not affected.

Reported by `@relkit/diagnostics`, `@relkit/observability`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
