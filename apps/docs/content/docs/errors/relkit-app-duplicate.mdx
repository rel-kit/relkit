**More than one app config is exported.**

## What happened

Your project exports more than one `defineApp` config. A RelKit app has exactly one.

## What to do

Keep a single `defineApp` export in `relkit.config.ts` and remove the others.

Reported by `@relkit/compiler`, `@relkit/diagnostics`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
