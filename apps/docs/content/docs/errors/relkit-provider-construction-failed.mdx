**Provider failed to start.**

## What happened

One of the app's providers threw an error or returned nothing while starting.

## What to do

Check the provider's settings and that the service it connects to is reachable, then restart the app.

Reported by `relkit/diagnostics`, `relkit/engine`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
