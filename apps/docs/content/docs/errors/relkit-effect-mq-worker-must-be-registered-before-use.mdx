**effect-mq worker registered too late.**

## What happened

An effect-mq worker was registered after the jobs client was already in use.

## What to do

Register the worker during startup, before any job is submitted or read.

Reported by `@relkit/effect-mq`, `relkit/diagnostics`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
