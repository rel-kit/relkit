**Inspector data is incomplete.**

## What happened

The Inspector dropped some records, for example because its buffers were full, so what it shows may have gaps.

## What to do

Treat the shown data as partial. Reduce how much the app records during inspection, or restart the inspection session.

Reported by `@relkit/diagnostics`, `@relkit/inspector`.
