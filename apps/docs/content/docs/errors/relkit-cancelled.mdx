**Operation was cancelled.**

## What happened

The operation stopped because its caller cancelled it or its abort signal fired before it finished.

## What to do

If you did not mean to cancel it, check where the abort signal comes from (a closed request, a client disconnect) and retry the operation.

Reported by `@relkit/diagnostics`, `@relkit/invocation`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
