**Task cannot wait for another task's result.**

## What happened

A task handler tried to wait for another job's result, which could block a worker indefinitely.

## What to do

Trigger the other job and finish this task, then handle its result in a follow-up job or event instead of waiting.

Reported by `@relkit/diagnostics`, `@relkit/jobs`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
