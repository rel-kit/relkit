**Task may not resume safely after a retry.**

## What happened

A task does outside work before a durable wait, or uses a wait key that can change between runs, so a retry may repeat work or lose its place.

## What to do

Derive wait keys from the task input or a versioned constant, and guard outside work with an idempotency key.

Reported by `@relkit/compiler`, `@relkit/diagnostics`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
