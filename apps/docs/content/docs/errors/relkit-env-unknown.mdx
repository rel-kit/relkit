**Unknown environment variable.**

## What happened

The variable you passed to `relkit env explain` is not declared in your environment definition.

## What to do

Run `relkit env list` to see the declared variables, then try again with one of those names.

Reported by `@relkit/cli`, `relkit/diagnostics`.
