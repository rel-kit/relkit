**Cache TTL is not valid.**

## What happened

A cache TTL is not a positive integer, is above the configured maximum, or `defaultTtlMs` is larger than `maxTtlMs`.

## What to do

Use a positive whole number of milliseconds for `ttlMs` that is not above `maxTtlMs`, or raise `maxTtlMs` in the cache definition.

Reported by `@relkit/cache`, `@relkit/diagnostics`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
