**Job access denied.**

## What happened

The caller is not allowed to see or change this job run, or the run belongs to another scope.

## What to do

Check the job's authorization rules and the scope your request resolves to. Make sure the caller is signed in with the right access.

Reported by `@relkit/client`, `@relkit/diagnostics`, `@relkit/jobs`, `@relkit/runtime-hono`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
