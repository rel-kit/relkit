**Job submission was cancelled.**

## What happened

The caller cancelled the submission before the job provider accepted it, so the job was not enqueued.

## What to do

Submit the job again if it is still needed, and check why the request or signal was cancelled.

Reported by `relkit/diagnostics`, `relkit/jobs`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
