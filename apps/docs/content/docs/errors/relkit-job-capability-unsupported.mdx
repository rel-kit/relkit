**Jobs service lacks a needed feature.**

## What happened

A task needs a feature, such as schedules or resources, that the selected jobs service does not offer.

## What to do

Choose a jobs service that supports the feature named in the error, or remove that requirement from the task.

Reported by `@relkit/compiler`, `@relkit/diagnostics`, `@relkit/jobs`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
