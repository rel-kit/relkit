**Jobs are not available in the Inspector.**

## What happened

The running app has no job service the Inspector can use.

## What to do

Make sure the app defines jobs and a jobs profile is configured in `relkit.config.ts`, then restart the app.

Reported by `relkit/diagnostics`, `relkit/inspector-api`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
