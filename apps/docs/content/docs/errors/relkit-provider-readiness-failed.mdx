**Provider health check failed.**

## What happened

A provider started but failed its health check, so the app did not start serving.

## What to do

Check that the service the provider connects to is running and reachable, then restart the app.

Reported by `relkit/diagnostics`, `relkit/engine`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
