**Retry settings are invalid.**

## What happened

A retry setting is missing where it is required, or one of its values is not allowed.

## What to do

Use a positive `maxAttempts`, a `multiplier` of at least 1, `jitter` of `"none"`, `"full"` or `"equal"`, and a `maxDelayMs` not below `initialDelayMs`.

Reported by `@relkit/compiler`, `@relkit/diagnostics`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
