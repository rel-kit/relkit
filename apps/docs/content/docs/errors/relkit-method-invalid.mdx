**HTTP method is invalid.**

## What happened

A route uses an HTTP method RelKit does not support.

## What to do

Use GET, POST, PUT, PATCH, DELETE, HEAD or OPTIONS.

Reported by `@relkit/compiler`, `@relkit/diagnostics`.
