**Model provider is not configured.**

## What happened

A model selector names a provider that is not configured in your app.

## What to do

Add the provider to your app's model settings, or change the selector to a configured provider.

Reported by `@relkit/compiler`, `relkit/agents`, `relkit/diagnostics`, `relkit/engine`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
