**Inspector is pointed at another environment.**

## What happened

The Inspector action was meant for a different environment than the one the app is running in.

## What to do

Connect the Inspector to the app in the environment you want to act on, then try again.

Reported by `relkit/diagnostics`, `relkit/inspector-api`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
