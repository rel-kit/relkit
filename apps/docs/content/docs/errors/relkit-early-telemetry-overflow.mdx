**Some early telemetry was dropped.**

## What happened

Your app produced more logs, spans or events than RelKit could hold before they were saved, so some records were dropped and the view is incomplete.

## What to do

Reduce logging during startup or bursts of work. Records that were dropped cannot be recovered.

Reported by `@relkit/diagnostics`, `@relkit/observability`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
