**Reloaded app returned an invalid health response.**

## What happened

During `relkit dev`, the reloaded app answered its health check with a response RelKit could not read.

## What to do

Make sure your app does not override RelKit's internal routes, then restart `relkit dev`. If it persists, report this on the RelKit issue tracker with the full output.

Reported by `@relkit/diagnostics`, `@relkit/supervisor`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
