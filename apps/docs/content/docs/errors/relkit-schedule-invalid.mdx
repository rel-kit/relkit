**Schedule is invalid.**

## What happened

A schedule is not in a list, repeats an ID, or lacks a five-field cron expression, a timezone, an overlap policy or JSON input.

## What to do

Give each schedule a unique `id`, a five-field `cron`, a `timezone`, `overlap` of `"skip"` or `"allow"`, and JSON `input`.

Reported by `@relkit/cli`, `relkit/diagnostics`, `relkit/providers-local`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
