**Singular job option is deprecated.**

## What happened

`defineApp` uses the singular `job` or `defaults.job` option. It still works for now but will be removed.

## What to do

Rename `job` to `jobs` and `defaults.job` to `defaults.jobs` in `defineApp`.

Reported by `@relkit/compiler`, `relkit/diagnostics`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
