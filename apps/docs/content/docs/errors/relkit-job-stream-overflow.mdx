**Job stream exceeded its limit.**

## What happened

A named job stream sent more frames or bytes, or a larger item, than the client allows.

## What to do

Send smaller or fewer stream items from the job, or raise the stream limits on the client.

Reported by `@relkit/client`, `@relkit/diagnostics`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
