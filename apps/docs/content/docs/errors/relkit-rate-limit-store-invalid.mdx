**Rate limit store is not a usable cache.**

## What happened

A route's rate limit `store` does not point at a cache that holds numbers.

## What to do

Point `store` at a cache defined with `defineCache` that stores numeric values.

Reported by `@relkit/compiler`, `relkit/diagnostics`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
