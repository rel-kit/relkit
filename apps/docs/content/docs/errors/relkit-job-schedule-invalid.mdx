**Job schedule is invalid.**

## What happened

A job's `schedules` option is not an array, repeats a schedule ID, or has a schedule without a valid `cron` or `every` value.

## What to do

Give each schedule a unique `id` and a valid `cron` or `every` value.

Reported by `@relkit/cli`, `relkit/diagnostics`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
