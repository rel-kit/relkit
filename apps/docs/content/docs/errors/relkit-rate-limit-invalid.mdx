**Rate limit settings are invalid.**

## What happened

A route's `rateLimit` needs a positive `limit`, a positive `windowMs` and a supported `key`, and one of these is missing or wrong.

## What to do

Set `limit` and `windowMs` to positive numbers and use a supported `key.kind`.

Reported by `@relkit/compiler`, `@relkit/diagnostics`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
