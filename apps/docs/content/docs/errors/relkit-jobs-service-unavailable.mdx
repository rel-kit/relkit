**Job service is not available.**

## What happened

The job provider reported that its service is not available, so the Inspector could not read job runs.

## What to do

Make sure the job provider is running and reachable, then refresh the Inspector.

Reported by `@relkit/diagnostics`, `@relkit/inspector-api`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
