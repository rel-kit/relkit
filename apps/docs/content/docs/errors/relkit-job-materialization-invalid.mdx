**Jobs could not be set up at startup.**

## What happened

When the app started, a job, queue or schedule pointed at something unknown, was registered twice, or had no queue or scheduler provider.

## What to do

Run `relkit check` to find the job problem, check the jobs provider in your app config, then rebuild with `relkit build`.

Reported by `relkit/diagnostics`, `relkit/engine`.

See [all error codes](/docs/errors).
