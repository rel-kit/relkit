Jobs now have a dedicated [getting-started guide](/docs/jobs). A job accepts validated work for later
execution by an ordinary function; acceptance is not completion. The job belongs to the domain that owns
the work, with queue policy separate from the function's business behavior.

## Choose delivery policy deliberately

The worker retries explicitly retryable declared application failures, not arbitrary exceptions.
[Retries and delivery](/docs/jobs/retries) covers attempt limits, timeouts, leases, and terminal work.
[Idempotency](/docs/jobs/idempotency) distinguishes local admission deduplication from repeated side effects
and current SQS behavior. Use [Schedules](/docs/jobs/schedules) for cron, timezone, and enqueue overlap.
