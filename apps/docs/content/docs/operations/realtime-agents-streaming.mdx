title: Realtime, agents, and streaming
description: Operate generations, limits, Redis state, and strict client deployments.
---

Reserve management capacity for health, cancellation, authorization renewal, and controls. Observation
does not consume finite function concurrency. A run remains bound to its original generation;
another generation may serve compatible journal reads and persist controls for the owner, but cannot
migrate execution or continuation. Forced retirement reports `GENERATION_UNAVAILABLE` without replay.

Deploy frontend and backend public-contract changes together. Even an additive public change blocks
an older browser under exact fingerprint matching. Show an application-updated state, preserve
unknown-operation metadata, and do not auto-reload while a mutation or control is unresolved.

Tune aggregate connection, partition, retained-byte, run, thread, and control limits before public
exposure. Per-principal overload is 429; application/provider overload is 503. Redis-backed guarantees
depend on persistence configuration, and provider resets must advance the provider epoch.
