<Callout type="note" title="Merge with your application" icon="settings">
  The root configuration helper is [`defineApp`](/docs/learn/concepts/application), not
  `defineConfig`. Merge the capability entries below into your existing `relkit.config.ts`; keep its
  ID, environment, profiles, and other settings. The empty `defineEnv({})` in a standalone example
  means this local provider needs no application variables. Keep your existing environment contract.
</Callout>
